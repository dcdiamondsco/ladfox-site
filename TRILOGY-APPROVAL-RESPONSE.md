## Approved oval-selection standard

Every centre diamond must provide excellent visual performance and be personally inspected by LADFOX. Selection must consider:

- minimal bow-tie;
- an attractive length-to-width ratio;
- properly balanced proportions;
- strong visual performance;
- overall appearance, rather than relying only on the grading certificate.

The precise numerical ratio is not currently prescribed; LADFOX retains professional selection discretion within this visual standard.
