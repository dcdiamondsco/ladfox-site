How we work

LADFOX provides remote bespoke jewellery design across the UK by email, WhatsApp and telephone. Engagement rings and other pieces are manufactured and hand-finished in Hatton Garden and qualifying pieces are hallmarked by the London Assay Office.

Orders and delivery

Immediate online checkout is available to UK customers only. International customers are welcome to request a separate quotation, subject to destination confirmation. The quotation will include agreed delivery, duties and taxes.
