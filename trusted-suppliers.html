Photographer & Videographer

Cassidy Storytelling

Cassidy Storytelling

Born and raised in North Yorkshire, Christian Cassidy built Cassidy Storytelling around a love of imagination, art, history and travel, then shaped that creative background through studies in electronic media and film production.

Since launching the business in spring 2017, he has produced wedding, commercial, theatre, sporting and charity work, with a clear focus on storytelling through photography and film. By 2024, Cassidy Storytelling had covered more than 400 weddings across the UK, Ireland and destination locations including New York, Amsterdam, Cyprus and Italy.

For couples who want wedding imagery that feels personal rather than formulaic, Cassidy Storytelling offers a thoughtful, experienced and visually driven approach.

Julie Bisacre

Her style is warm, professional and light-hearted, helping couples feel genuinely at ease while she guides them through the moments that matter most. The emphasis is always on creating a celebration that feels unmistakably like your day and your love story.

Drinks Service

The Champagne Donkey

The Champagne Donkey

The Champagne Donkey offers something genuinely different for weddings and events through Mrs Durrell, a Mediterranean miniature donkey who serves drinks from custom saddle baskets while moving through the celebration.

Her calm, friendly nature makes her a natural talking point as well as a memorable photo opportunity, adding a playful and distinctive layer to the guest experience rather than a standard drinks service.

After four years exclusively delighting guests at Hillfields Farm wedding venue, Mrs Durrell is now available more widely for couples who want a whimsical detail their guests are unlikely to forget.
