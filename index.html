Before you start

How much does a bespoke ring cost?

Bespoke engagement rings ordinarily start from £1,999. Your design, metal and stones determine the final price. We’ll provide a quote before you decide to go ahead.

What is the Ring Collection?

A curated selection of made-to-order solitaire, trilogy and lab gemstone rings. Choose a design, customise the available details and see the price online. For a completely original piece, start with a complimentary initial design.

Do I need a finished design?

No. Start with a rough idea, a screenshot or a budget. Your initial design is complimentary, and our family-run team will help you work out the details.

When do I pay, and when will I see the CAD?

We start with a complimentary initial design and a quote. Once you choose to go ahead and make payment, we provide your CAD design within a week.

Can I make changes before the ring is made?

Yes. We’ll adjust the CAD with you until you’re happy with the design. Your approval comes before production begins.

How long will my ring take?

Production can take up to five weeks depending on complexity. Allow time before that for your initial design, quote and any CAD adjustments.

What warranty and delivery are included?

Your bespoke engagement ring comes with a lifetime manufacturing warranty and insured, tracked delivery. Read the full terms and exclusions.
