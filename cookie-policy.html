Cookie policy

LADFOX uses limited browser storage for essential site functions and optional advertising measurement.

Last updated: 18 September 2026.

Essential storage

The website may store your privacy choice, temporary form progress, attribution needed to complete an enquiry and a record used to prevent a confirmed purchase being counted repeatedly. This supports functions you request and cannot always be disabled through our banner.

Optional Google advertising measurement

With your permission, the Google tag may measure visits and actions such as successful enquiries, telephone or WhatsApp clicks, checkout starts and confirmed purchases. It may also connect an advertising click identifier with a later action. LADFOX does not intentionally send names, email addresses, telephone numbers, message content or uploaded images in these measurement events.

Consent Mode

Optional advertising storage, analytics storage, advertising user data and advertising personalisation are denied by default. If you accept optional tracking, your preference is saved in your browser and the Google tag receives the updated choice.

Changing your choice

You can clear this site’s storage in your browser to display the choice again. You may also use browser controls to block or delete cookies. Declining optional tracking does not stop you using the website or contacting LADFOX.
