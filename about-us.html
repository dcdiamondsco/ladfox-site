The Story

A better alternative to traditional jewellery retail in London

Ladfox was created to offer a more thoughtful way to buy fine jewellery. Too often, traditional jewellery retail relies on inflated pricing, limited guidance, and designs shaped around what is already in stock rather than what is actually right for the person wearing it.

For something as significant as an engagement ring, that approach can feel impersonal and rushed. The process should be clear, informed, and built around the individual piece being made.

Ladfox was established to do that properly. Instead of pushing standard retail lines, we focus on bespoke design, carefully selected diamonds, and measured advice that helps clients make confident decisions without noise or pressure.

The Approach

Each bespoke ring begins with the details that actually matter

Every Ladfox piece starts with a conversation. We take the time to understand what you want visually, how the ring needs to sit and wear, and which details will shape the final result in a meaningful way.

From there, diamonds are sourced and assessed individually. We pay close attention to factors that affect how a stone performs in real life, including proportions, light return, overall balance, and in shapes such as ovals, bow tie minimisation. A certificate matters, but it does not tell the whole story.

Once the right stone is selected, the ring is designed around it. Setting style, proportions, band width, and finer elements are considered together so the finished piece feels resolved, balanced, and intentional rather than generic.

Throughout the process, guidance remains personal and direct. You are not handed from person to person or pushed towards a stock option. The experience is designed to feel private, straightforward, and properly looked after from start to finish.
