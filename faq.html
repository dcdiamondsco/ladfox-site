General Questions

A clearer overview of what Ladfox is, how the process works, and what clients can expect before they enquire.

What is Ladfox, and what kind of jeweller are you?

Ladfox is a private Hatton Garden jeweller specialising in bespoke engagement rings, custom jewellery, and carefully sourced diamonds. The process is more direct and personal than traditional retail, with each piece shaped around the client, the selected stone, and the final result rather than stock already sitting in a cabinet.

What makes Ladfox different from a high street jeweller or a typical online ring retailer?

The main difference is that the process is not built around volume. Many traditional jewellers rely on mass-produced settings, limited stock ranges, or generic sales language. Ladfox takes a more selective approach, with more attention paid to diamond performance, design balance, wearability, and the smaller details that shape the finished piece.

Do you only make engagement rings, or can you help with other bespoke jewellery as well?

Engagement rings are a major part of what we do, but not the only focus. Ladfox can also help with other custom jewellery projects where a client wants a more tailored result than a standard retail piece.

Can I still enquire if I do not fully know what I want yet?

Yes. Many clients get in touch before they know the exact shape, setting, size, or final brief. A loose idea, a few screenshots, or even just a budget range is enough to begin. From there, the process becomes about narrowing choices intelligently rather than guessing.

How do I start a bespoke engagement ring project with Ladfox?

The simplest route is to make an enquiry with your rough idea, references, timeline, and budget range. From there, the brief can be refined, diamond options can be discussed, and the design can start taking shape in a more structured way.

Diamonds and Design

Questions about lab-grown diamonds, certificates, visual performance, and the details that usually matter more than people expect.

Do you only offer lab-grown diamonds, or can you advise more broadly depending on the brief?

Lab-grown diamonds are a major focus because they often offer excellent value and allow clients to achieve a stronger combination of size, quality, and design without drifting into inflated budgets. If a client wants to compare options or discuss a different requirement, that can be handled directly.

Are lab-grown diamonds real diamonds?

Yes. Lab-grown diamonds are real diamonds, with the same physical and chemical properties as mined diamonds. The key difference is origin rather than appearance.

Do diamond certificates tell the whole story, or are there things they do not show clearly?

A certificate is useful, but it is not the full story. It gives a baseline for colour, clarity, measurements, and other core grading information, but it does not fully explain how a diamond will look in motion, in daylight, or once it is set. Two stones with very similar paperwork can still perform very differently in real life.

What is the bow-tie effect in oval diamonds?

The bow-tie effect is a shadow across the centre of many oval, pear, and marquise diamonds. Some is normal, but excessive bow tie can make a stone look darker and less lively. It is one of the clearest examples of why visual selection matters beyond the certificate.

Why do proportions and light performance matter so much?

Because they affect how bright, balanced, and lively a diamond actually looks. A stone can look acceptable on paper while still appearing flat, dark, or poorly spread in real life. Strong proportions and light performance usually make more visible difference than chasing small grading upgrades that are hard to see once worn.

Can you help if I already have screenshots, inspiration images, or a rough idea of the style I want?

Yes. Reference images are often one of the most useful starting points because they help identify what you actually like. They are used as a base for refining the design rather than copying an image blindly.

Can you design a ring to sit flush with a wedding band?

Yes. If flush fit matters to you, it should be considered early. Setting height, basket shape, and band position all affect whether a wedding ring can sit neatly alongside the engagement ring without a gap.

Can I use my own stone for a bespoke ring?

In many cases, yes. The stone would usually need to be assessed first so the design can be built around its condition, proportions, and suitability for the setting style you want.

Pricing, Timelines and Delivery

Practical questions about budgets, lead times, insured UK delivery, and what happens once the piece is complete.

What budget do clients usually spend, and how far can a realistic budget go with Ladfox?

Many Ladfox clients spend between £1,500 and £5,000, although the right budget depends on the centre stone, metal, and design ambition. The aim is to use the budget intelligently, with more attention paid to the details that genuinely improve the finished piece rather than simply pushing spend higher.

Are bespoke engagement rings always more expensive than ready-made rings?

Not necessarily. In many cases, bespoke can offer better value because the budget is being allocated more carefully. The finished piece is also built around your priorities rather than the compromises of standard retail stock.

How long does a bespoke ring usually take, and when should I get in touch if I have a deadline?

Bespoke pieces are usually completed within a few weeks, depending on the stone, design complexity, and whether there is a fixed proposal date or deadline. If timing matters, it is best to mention that as early as possible so the process can be structured around it properly.

Do you deliver across the UK, and how is the ring sent once it is finished?

Yes. Completed pieces can be sent across the UK using insured and tracked delivery so the process remains secure from dispatch through to arrival.

Can you help if I need the ring for a proposal on a specific date?

Yes, provided the timeline is realistic. The earlier that date is mentioned, the easier it is to advise clearly and manage the process without unnecessary pressure near the end.

Do your rings come with a warranty or aftercare support once the project is complete?

Yes. Ladfox provides a lifetime warranty along with ongoing support after the ring is completed, so clients still have direct help available after delivery.

Practical Questions

Useful points around ring size, wearability, proposals, and what to do if you are still at an early stage.

What if I do not know my partner’s ring size?

That is very common. An estimated size is usually enough to begin, and most rings can be resized afterwards if needed. It is usually better to keep the proposal moving than to delay everything trying to get an exact size too early.

Can you help me keep the ring a surprise?

Yes. That often comes down to simple practical planning, such as timing, delivery arrangements, and how communication is handled during the process.

Can I enquire even if I am only comparing options at this stage?

Yes. In fact, it is often useful to ask questions before committing elsewhere. A short conversation can help clarify what matters, what does not, and whether a particular budget or design direction is realistic.

What should I send when I make an enquiry?

The most useful starting points are reference images, a rough budget, any preferred stone shape, a ring size if you have one, and any key deadline such as a proposal date. Even if some of that is missing, a brief message is still enough to begin.
